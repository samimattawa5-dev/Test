# Monetization & copyright (researched 2026-11-15; secondary sources — verify in each app's official page)

## YouTube (YPP)
- Full ads: 1,000 subs + (4,000 watch hrs/12 mo OR 10M Shorts views/90 days).
- Entry tier (fan funding): ~500 subs + 3 public uploads/90d + 3M Shorts views/90d (or 3,000 hrs).
- Baseline: AdSense linked, 2-Step Verification, no active strikes, eligible country, enable Shorts monetization in Studio.
- Change coming 2027-02-01: thresholds ~double for NEW creators (8,000 hrs / 20M Shorts views) → join before.
- Inauthentic/mass-produced/repetitive content demonetizes the whole channel (AI allowed). Make each episode unique: own script, voice, story.
- Disclose realistic synthetic media.

## TikTok (Creator Rewards)
- 10,000 real followers + 100,000 views/30 days, age 18+, video must be >=60s (Shorts under 1 min don't earn), limited countries (US, UK, FR, DE, JP, KR, BR reported — check in app).
- Label AI-generated realistic content.

## Instagram
- Professional account, 18+, Partner Monetization Policies. Subscriptions ~10k followers; Gifts ~500; bonuses invite-only (varies by country). Check Professional Dashboard.

## Copyright checklist per video
- Models: CC0 (confirm license page of the exact pack; CC-BY needs credit; skip "editorial").
- Music/SFX: only CC0, platform-library, or paid-license. No pop songs.
- Voice: own, or licensed TTS (check terms). Own scripts/art = original.
- Keep license proof (URL/screenshot) in /licenses.

## Strategy
Make ~60-70s episodes: qualifies TikTok (>60s) and Shorts/Reels; post same video on all 3.
