## Fact list

Primary study: M. Bauch & U. Büntgen, "Climate-driven changes in Mediterranean grain trade mitigated famine but introduced the Black Death to medieval Europe", *Communications Earth & Environment* 6 (Dec 2025), DOI 10.1038/s43247-025-02964-0.

1. **2025 study by Bauch & Büntgen** (Leibniz Institute GWZO and University of Cambridge) proposes that a volcanic eruption around 1345 was likely the first step in a chain that led to the Black Death. This is a proposed chain of events, a hypothesis. Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find), [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1107493)
2. **"An eruption, or series of eruptions", around 1345, volcano unknown, likely tropical.** Ice cores show a sulphur spike around 1345. Bauch: "We cannot say very much about the volcanic eruption." No volcano has been identified. Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2025/12/volcanic-eruption-set-the-stage-for-the-black-death-researchers-find/), [BBC Science Focus](https://www.sciencefocus.com/news/massive-volcanic-eruptions-black-death)
3. **Up to 60% died in some regions.** This is a regional maximum, not the figure for all of Europe; voice and counter must keep "up to" and "in some regions". Sources: [BBC Science Focus](https://www.sciencefocus.com/news/massive-volcanic-eruptions-black-death), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2025/12/volcanic-eruption-set-the-stage-for-the-black-death-researchers-find/), [KTVZ/CNN](https://ktvz.com/?p=1588763)
4. **Pale "blue rings" in Spanish Pyrenees trees mean unusually cold, wet summers.** The years are given as 1345, 1346 and 1347 in some sources and as 1345 and 1346 in others, so the video shows no years for this clue. Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/biology/volcanic-eruptions-black-death), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2025/12/volcanic-eruption-set-the-stage-for-the-black-death-researchers-find/)
5. **Writers at the time described unusual cloudiness and dark lunar eclipses.** Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1107493)
6. **The cooling could have led to widespread crop failure across the Mediterranean.** Keep "could have". Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find)
7. **In 1347 Venice, Genoa and Pisa imported grain from the Black Sea** (the Golden Horde, around the Sea of Azov). The imports eased the famine, which is the "mitigated famine" in the paper's title. Sources: [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/), [Scimex](https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/did-volcanic-activity-related-famine-set-the-black-death-in-motion-in-europe), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find)
8. **The first human plague cases in Venice came less than two months after the last grain ships arrived.** Some outlets say "a few weeks", so "less than two months" covers both. Sources: [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/), [Scimex](https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/did-volcanic-activity-related-famine-set-the-black-death-in-motion-in-europe), [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/12/251208014623.htm)
9. **The ships most likely carried fleas infected with *Yersinia pestis*, feeding on grain dust.** Bauch says rat fleas can survive for months on grain dust. Keep "most likely". Sources: [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/12/251208014623.htm), [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find)
10. **"Many Italian cities, even large ones like Milan and Rome, were most probably not affected by the Black Death, apparently because they did not need to import grain after 1345"** (Bauch, quoted word for word). Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1107493)
11. **Debate: other historians are cautious.** Some say the coverage overstated the study, and gaps in the records may explain why some towns show no imports or no early plague. Monica Green (not involved) found the volcanic data helpful but notes the epidemic also depended on existing rodent and insect reservoirs. Sources: [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find), [Modern Medieval](https://buttondown.com/ModernMedieval/archive/a-volcano-caused-the-black-death), [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/)
