## Fact list

Primary study: M. Bauch & U. Büntgen, "Climate-driven changes in Mediterranean grain trade mitigated famine but introduced the Black Death to medieval Europe", *Communications Earth & Environment* 6 (Dec 2025), DOI 10.1038/s43247-025-02964-0.

1. **2025 study by Bauch & Büntgen** (Leibniz Institute GWZO and University of Cambridge) proposes that a volcanic eruption around 1345 was likely the first step in a chain that led to the Black Death. This is a proposed chain of events, a hypothesis. Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find), [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1107493)
2. **"An eruption, or series of eruptions", around 1345, volcano unknown, likely tropical.** Ice cores show a sulphur spike around 1345. Bauch: "We cannot say very much about the volcanic eruption." No volcano has been identified. Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2025/12/volcanic-eruption-set-the-stage-for-the-black-death-researchers-find/), [BBC Science Focus](https://www.sciencefocus.com/news/massive-volcanic-eruptions-black-death)

Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [New Atlas](https://newatlas.com/biology/volcanic-eruptions-black-death), [Medievalists.net](https://www.medievalists.net/2025/12/volcanic-eruption-set-the-stage-for-the-black-death-researchers-find/)
5. **Writers at the time described unusual cloudiness and dark lunar eclipses.** Sources: [Cambridge](https://www.cam.ac.uk/stories/volcanoes-black-death), [EurekAlert](https://www.eurekalert.org/news-releases/1107493)

Sources: [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/), [Scimex](https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/did-volcanic-activity-related-famine-set-the-black-death-in-motion-in-europe), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find)

Sources: [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/), [Scimex](https://www.scimex.org/newsfeed/did-volcanic-activity-related-famine-set-the-black-death-in-motion-in-europe), [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/12/251208014623.htm)

Sources: [ScienceDaily](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/12/251208014623.htm), [The Debrief](https://thedebrief.org/volcanoes-famine-and-grain-ships-how-climate-driven-trade-may-have-brought-the-black-death-to-europe/), [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/planet-earth/volcanic-eruption-triggered-butterfly-effect-that-led-to-the-black-death-researchers-find)
10. **"Many Italian cities, even large ones like Milan and Rome, were most probably not affected by the Black Death, apparently because they did not need to import grain after 1345"** (Bauch, quoted word for word).
